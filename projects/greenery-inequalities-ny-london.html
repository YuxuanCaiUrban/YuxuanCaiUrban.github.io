Greenery Inequalities in New York and London: Socio-economic and Spatial Dimensions

Hu, Y., Chen, M., & Cai, Y.

Green EquityComparative CitiesSpatial Analysis

Abstract

Compares how access to urban greenery is distributed across New York and London, examining the socio-economic and spatial dimensions along which that access is unequal.

Approach

Running the same analysis across two cities makes it possible to separate patterns specific to one urban context from those that recur across them.
