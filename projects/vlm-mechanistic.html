What Do Vision-Language Models Know About Urban Perception? A Mechanistic Investigation

Cai, Y.

VLMsInterpretabilityUrban Perception

Abstract

Investigated the internal neuron activations of vision-language models to reveal how representations of urban safety, walkability, and gentrification emerge inside large multi-modal networks.

Approach

Rather than treating the model as a black-box scorer, the analysis works at the level of individual activations, asking which internal features a model actually uses when it judges an urban scene.
